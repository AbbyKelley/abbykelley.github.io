ABOUT ME

Hello, I’m Abby and I use my background in art history and a love for clear organization to help create experiences! I believe that the most compelling work requires a foundation of careful planning and a trained visual aesthetic. While my degree in Art History at St Andrews taught me to evaluate culture, my mind naturally leans toward organization and hard data leading me to an MSc in Strategic Marketing at Imperial College, London. I draw constant inspiration from contemporary art and live music, yet my absolute focus remains firmly rooted in fashion. I am captivated by fashion photography and physical garment display, exploring these themes extensively in my undergraduate dissertation by analyzing the ethics of brand-funded museum exhibitions alongside the critical need for proper contextualization in fashion displays. Because my creative background is paired with an analytical mindset, I rely on clear frameworks to guide my decisions. Ultimately, I merge this understanding of visual narrative with administrative control to help construct memorable fashion experiences.
